# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, engineering leaders, technical recruiters, and peers who need to understand Zach Parent's craft quickly. They arrive from a resume, LinkedIn, referral, or direct portfolio link and are scanning for credibility, taste, and a clear way to make contact.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio home page introduces Zach as a software developer with a reliable systems mindset and a polished product sensibility. Success means a visitor immediately understands the role, current work, education/project history, location, and contact path without feeling like they are reading a generic resume template.

## Brand Personality

Precise, humane, and quietly technical. The page should feel like a field note from a developer who values craft: measured grid lines, clear type, restrained motion, real portraiture, and small interactive details that reward attention.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing pages, identical card grids, purple-blue AI gradients, decorative glass panels, neon terminal cosplay, stock-avatar hero sections, and over-explained in-app text. Avoid motion that delays reading or feels playful at the expense of professionalism.

## Design Principles

- Keep the blueprint spirit: visible grid, measurement marks, and code fragments should feel intentional, not decorative filler.
- Let the portrait anchor the page. The human signal matters as much as the technical system.
- Prefer tight information density with enough breathing room for scanning.
- Use motion as evidence of care: short, purposeful, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
- Make responsive behavior feel designed at every width, not simply scaled down.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text, focus states, and interactive controls. Maintain keyboard access for all navigation and theme controls. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keep image framing stable and square so content does not jump between breakpoints or themes.
